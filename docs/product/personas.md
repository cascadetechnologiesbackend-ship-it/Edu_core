# SchoolMitra Core Personas

SchoolMitra is designed strictly around the real days, constraints, and finished jobs of six human personas in mid-size Indian K-12 schools (500–2,000 students).

---

## 1. Office Admin (`office_admin`)
- **Who**: The school office clerk — lowest patience, highest workload, decides daily whether the software helps or gets bypassed.
- **Real Day**: 7:40 AM gate duty, followed by counter fee queries, absentee phone chasing, admission walk-ins during season, and transport calls. The day is non-stop interruptions.
- **Primary Jobs**:
  1. Collect and account for today's fees.
  2. Chase today's absentees before parents panic.
  3. Move admission applicants through the admissions funnel.
  4. Answer any parent inquiry in under one minute.
- **Moment of Delight**: At 3:00 PM, the day's money is reconciled, and the defaulter list already knows who to call.

---

## 2. Accountant (`accountant`)
- **Who**: Runs the ledger, fears statutory audit season, does not trust software until it survives one real month-end close.
- **Real Day**: Voucher entry, gateway settlement reconciliation, expense approvals, month-end ledger close, and fee reconciliation against the physical bank statement.
- **Primary Jobs**:
  1. Reconcile every payment against the bank and payment gateway.
  2. Close the month with balanced reports that the school trustee and CA accept.
  3. Survive a statutory audit without spending weeks assembling paper files.
- **Moment of Delight**: Month-end close is a single verified flow, with double-entry accounting invariants pre-checked and green.

---

## 3. Principal (`principal`)
- **Who**: Accountable for everything, present in nothing, wants one honest answer weekly.
- **Real Day**: Morning assembly, corridor walkthroughs, parent confrontations, board correspondence, and managing teacher staffing gaps.
- **Primary Jobs**:
  1. Answer with certainty: *Are we on track across money, attendance, and syllabus pace?*
  2. Spot the one section or teacher requiring administrative intervention this week.
  3. Report to the school governing trust with verified, unvarnished numbers.
- **Moment of Delight**: One single screen on Monday morning, showing three core numbers, all strictly true.

---

## 4. Classroom Teacher (`teacher`)
- **Who**: Has a 40-minute period, and the software gets at most four minutes of it.
- **Real Day**: 6 teaching periods, attendance roster at each period, marks entry windows, homework assignments, PTM scheduling, and substitution periods.
- **Primary Jobs**:
  1. Mark section attendance in under four minutes.
  2. Post homework before leaving the classroom.
  3. Enter term marks within the prescribed window.
  4. Glance at today's schedule and weekly timetable.
- **Moment of Delight**: Attendance confirmed in the room, and homework posted in three taps from the podium before the bell rings.

---

## 5. Parent (`parent`)
- **Who**: Operates the app during a 90-second tea break; will immediately call the school office if the app fails even once.
- **Real Day**: Morning drop-off glance, fee reminders, absence notification if marked absent, homework check at 9:00 PM, and PTM booking once a term.
- **Primary Jobs**:
  1. Pay term fees in under 90 seconds.
  2. Confirm child was marked present today.
  3. See tonight's homework and what topics were covered.
  4. Receive official school circulars without calling the front desk.
- **Moment of Delight**: Absence alert arrives before the worry sets in, and paying fees takes less time than finding their card.

---

## 6. Student (`student`)
- **Who**: Accesses via a shared or personal phone at 9:00 PM, homework due tomorrow, seeks academic dignity rather than surveillance.
- **Real Day**: Regular school periods, homework assignments, checking marks, and verifying tomorrow's schedule.
- **Primary Jobs**:
  1. View assignments due today.
  2. Submit homework cleanly and honestly.
  3. See exam marks and feedback.
  4. View tomorrow's timetable and required books.
- **Moment of Delight**: Homework submitted in three taps, and marked late only when it was genuinely late.

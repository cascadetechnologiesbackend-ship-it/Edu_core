# SchoolMitra Product Thesis

## Statement
SchoolMitra is the operating system for an Indian K-12 school: the fee ledger is legally auditable, the data always tells the truth, and parents open an app instead of calling the office. It wins on trust, financial depth, and honest UX — never on price.

## Why It Wins
The codebase already earns this thesis, and most competitors cannot copy it without complete re-architecture:
- **DPDP-Native Compliance**: Consent management, data fiduciary rights, breach incident handling, and automated data retention policies built into the data layer.
- **Security & Privacy**: AES-256-GCM encryption for student and guardian PII at rest, with strict tenant isolation (`withTenant`) and row-level security.
- **Financial Depth**: True double-entry accounting with balanced journal invariants, automated gateway settlement splits, and complete audit trails.
- **Honesty Scanners & Certified Pipelines**: Automated static analysis preventing fabricated figures, hardcoded placeholders, or silent failures.
- **Bilingual, Offline-First Role PWAs**: English and Hindi support across all role surfaces with resilient offline queueing and background synchronization.

## Who Pays
School trusts, managing committees, and administrators of mid-size CBSE, ICSE, and state-board schools with 500 to 2,000 enrolled students in our beachhead market.

## What They Buy
Not software licenses or feature menus. They buy **finished days**:
- Daily fees collected and reconciled by 3:00 PM.
- Classroom attendance marked in under four minutes per section.
- The monthly financial ledger closed with verified accounting invariants.
- Examination results published to parents without errors or delays.
- Parents informed in real time without a single untracked phone call to the front office.

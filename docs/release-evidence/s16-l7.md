   - Queries strictly `{ studentId: feeInvoices.studentId, status: feeInvoices.status }` from `feeInvoices`.
   - Never selects, projects, or leaks fee amounts, ledger lines, invoice balances, or payment records.
   - Maps status strictly to categorical enum `"PAID" | "DUE" | "OVERDUE"`.

2. **Role-Scoped Fee Decoupling in Student 360**:
   - In `getTeacherStudent360Drawer`:
     - If `accessLevel === "FULL_HOMEROOM"`: Categorical status badge (`PAID` | `DUE` | `OVERDUE`) is projected.

     - Exam marks and homework evaluations are strictly filtered to the teacher's taught subjects for non-homeroom sections.

## 1. Executive Summary & Walkthrough Narrative

Sprint S21 closes the foundational gap between a provisioned school digital twin and an operationally executable school ready to conduct Day One.

Prior to S21, blueprint provisioning populated structural entities (classes, subjects, fee heads) but left the school inoperable:
- Universal import commit was a simulated `setTimeout(800)` that wrote zero rows.
- Blueprints contained bell schedules, but the provisioning engine never cloned them to `bellSchedule`.
- The deterministic timetable generator in `timetableEngine.ts` had zero callers.
- The setup health score counted entity presence and falsely reported 100% for a school with no bell schedule, no timetable, no teacher allocations, unpriced fee structures, and zero students.
- `setupSchool` permitted duplicate active academic years.

In Sprint S21, all operational paths were connected, audited, and tested end-to-end without simulation compromises.

## 2. Walkthrough Steps & Verification Evidence

### Step 1: Idempotent Blueprint Bell Schedule Provisioning & Top-Up
- **Surface**: Super-Admin Schools Console (`/super-admin/schools`)
- **Server Action**: `topUpSchoolProvisionAction(schoolId)` & `executeProvisionSchool`
- **Result**:
  - Cloned 8 daily bell schedule periods (Period 1 through 8, plus Assembly and Lunch breaks) into `bellSchedule` table with active tenant scope.
  - Calling `topUpSchoolProvision` on an existing provisioned school idempotently seeds missing bell schedule rows without re-provisioning or duplicating existing classes/subjects.
- **Verification Evidence**: `src/__tests__/s21-bell-schedule-adversarial.test.ts` (15/15 passed).

### Step 2: Universal Data Import Real Database Commits (Students & Staff)
- **Surface**: Universal Import Center (`/school/import-center` / `ImportExportModal.tsx`)
- **Server Actions**: `commitStudentImportAction` & `commitStaffImportAction`
- **Invariants Enforced**:
  - Direct atomic transactions (`db.transaction`).
  - Student creation delegates strictly to canonical `createImportedStudent` (`backend/src/lib/studentLifecycleEngine.ts`), guaranteeing 0 lifecycle hygiene violations.
  - Staff creation generates valid `persons`, `staff`, and `users` records with proper encryption attributes (`dateOfBirthEncrypted`, `genderEncrypted`) and role clamping (`TEACHER`, `STAFF`, `ACCOUNTANT`).
  - Batch size capped at 5,000 rows. All-or-nothing rollback on validation failure.
  - Line-numbered rejected-rows CSV downloads generated on client/server failure.
- **Verification Evidence**: `src/__tests__/s21-import-commit.test.ts` (9/9 passed), `src/__tests__/s21-m2-adversarial-challenger.test.ts` (30/30 passed).

### Step 3: Deterministic Timetable Generator & Conflict-Free Auto-Assignment
- **Surface**: Timetable Builder (`/academics?tab=timetable` / `QuickTimetable.tsx`)
- **Server Actions**: `generateSectionTimetablePreview` & `commitSectionTimetable`
- **Migration**: `0057_timetable_manual_override.sql` adding `isManualOverride` boolean to `timetablePeriods`.
- **Invariants Enforced**:
  - Mathematical workload fit validated before schedule synthesis.
  - Anti-fatigue constraint: max 2 periods of the same subject per day.
  - Teacher conflict detection: zero double-booking across concurrent sections.
  - Dedicated 3-tier modal: Preview $\rightarrow$ Conflict Audit $\rightarrow$ Atomic Commit.
  - Manual overrides flagged with `isManualOverride: true` and strictly preserved across regeneration runs.
- **Verification Evidence**: `src/__tests__/s21-timetable-wiring.test.ts` (10/10 passed), `src/__tests__/s21-timetable-challenge.test.ts` (11/11 passed), `src/__tests__/s21-m3-challenger2.test.tsx` (9/9 passed).

### Step 4: Mobile 390px Viewport Zero-Horizontal-Overflow Reflow

### Step 5: Honest Setup Health Score & Dual-Score Display
- **Surface**: School Setup Hub (`/settings/school-setup`) & Settings Dashboard (`/settings`)
- **Server Action**: `getSchoolConfigurationHealthAction`
- **Dual Score Telemetry**:
  1. `configurationScore` (6 static foundations: Academic Year, Classes, Subjects, Fee Heads, Salary Grades, Holidays).
  2. `operationalScore` (5 Day-One execution gates: Bell Periods > 0, 100% Timetable Coverage, Fee Heads Priced > 0, Teacher Allocations > 0, Enrolled Students > 0).
- **Honesty Invariants Enforced**:
  - Blueprint-provisioned school reports 100% `configurationScore` and 0% `operationalScore` until operational entities exist.
  - Zero-price fee heads strictly clamp status to `PARTIAL`, never `COMPLETE`, surfacing Next-Best-Action link to `/finance/fees`.
  - `isOperationallyReady` requires strictly 100% operational score (all 5 milestones green).
- **Verification Evidence**: `src/__tests__/s21-operational-readiness.test.ts` (15/15 passed).

### Step 6: Academic Year Singularity Guard

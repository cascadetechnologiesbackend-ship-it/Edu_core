## 1. Objectives & Changes Made

1. **Wired S24-F03 (InsightCard Dead Branch)**:
   - In `backend/src/lib/teacherEventEngine/snapshotAssembler.ts`, implemented Section 13 to assemble `insights`.
   - Populated `snapshot.insights` across the three designed analytical models under **OD2**:
     - **Model 1: `section_attendance_trend`**:
       - Evaluated on class-teacher sections.
       - Sufficiency gate: Requires at least 5 past school days of attendance history.
       - Trigger: Today's section attendance rate dips by $\ge 15$ percentage points below the trailing 5-day average.
       - Emits sparkline data array and average rate; silent when history is insufficient or dip is $<15\%$.
     - **Model 2: `homework_neglect_list`**:
       - Evaluated across published assignments in the past 14 days.
       - Sufficiency gate: Requires at least 3 published assignments.
       - Trigger: Identifies students who have missed $\ge 3$ homework assignments.
       - Resolves real student decrypted names via `batchResolveStudentIdentities`.
       - Silent when fewer than 3 assignments exist or zero students meet the threshold.
     - **Model 3: `pre_publish_distribution`**:
       - Evaluated on marks entry windows closing within 48 hours.
       - Sufficiency gate: Requires at least 60% of student marks entered and at least 3 scores.
       - Computes mean score, max marks, and identifies low/high outliers ($\le 20\%$ or $\ge 95\%$).
       - Silent when completion rate is $<60\%$ or window is not closing within 48h.
2. **Honesty Law Invariance**:
   - Every model fails closed: if data is insufficient, it produces zero cards rather than degraded or invented estimates. The absence of an insight is asserted in automated tests.

## 1. Summary of Changes

Layer L2 addresses flaws **S19-F02** (Lack of an active Now-Cockpit in Teacher App), **S19-F08** (Unmarked past periods leave no operational trace), and **S19-F09** (Wide tables force horizontal scrolling on mobile viewports).

### A. Now-Cockpit Card & Day Progress Ring (`NowNextFeedCard.tsx` & `deriveTeacherFeed.ts`)
- In `backend/src/lib/teacherEventEngine/deriveTeacherFeed.ts`:
  - Enriched `NowNextCard` payload with `totalPeriodsCount`, `completedPeriodsCount`, and `minutesRemaining`.
- In `frontend/src/components/teacher/cards/NowNextFeedCard.tsx`:
  - Upgraded card to full Now-Cockpit (`data-testid="now-cockpit-card"`).
  - Added day progress ring (`data-testid="now-cockpit-progress-ring"`) with circular SVG progress tracking `{completedPeriodsCount} of {totalPeriodsCount}` teaching periods completed.
  - Added live countdown badge (`{minutesRemaining}m left`) and running period indicator.
  - Added embedded 1-tap roll call launcher (`data-testid="now-cockpit-rollcall-btn"`) routing directly to `/teacher/attendance?sectionId=${sectionId}` (Target: 1 tap).
  - Added quick-shortcuts rail linking directly to Homework (`/teacher/homework`), Classes & Rosters (`/teacher/classes`), and Syllabus Tracker (`/teacher/syllabus`).

### B. Unmarked Past Periods Pending Chips (`classesActions.ts` & `MyDayTimeline.tsx`)
- In `classesActions.ts`:
  - Added `isAttendanceMarked?: boolean | undefined` to `MyDayPeriodItem`.
  - In `getTeacherMyDayTimeline`, queried `studentAttendance` for `targetDateStr` and cross-referenced against period `sectionId`.
- In `MyDayTimeline.tsx`:
  - Added pending-period chip (`data-testid="pending-attendance-chip"`): If a past instructional period lacks attendance records, renders *"Attendance Pending / उपस्थिति लंबित"* with a 1-tap link directly into attendance marking for that section.

### C. Wide-Table Mobile Reflow (`HomeroomWorkspaceView.tsx`)
- Enforced the Universal Scroll & Click Law (zero horizontal overflow at 390px viewport width).
- In `HomeroomWorkspaceView.tsx`:
  - For viewports `< 768px` (`block md:hidden`), reflowed the multi-column report card matrix into a responsive card-per-student layout.
  - Each mobile student card features:
    - Name (clickable for Student 360 profile drawer), Admission number, Total marks, Percentage badge.
    - Responsive 2-column grid of subject score pills, cleanly distinguishing teacher's own subjects.
    - Inline Conduct Grade dropdown, remarks input, and 1-tap "Sign Off" button.
  - Desktop multi-column table preserved for `>= 768px` (`hidden md:block`).

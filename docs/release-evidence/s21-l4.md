## 1. Scope & Delivered Features

- **F14: Operational Readiness Health Score (`getSchoolConfigurationHealthAction`)**:
  - Implemented dual-score calculation separating static configuration completeness (`configurationScore`, 6 milestones) from true day-one operational readiness (`operationalScore`, 5 milestones).
  - 5 Operational Milestones:
    1. `bellSchedulePeriods > 0`: Active instructional periods in bell schedule.
    2. `sectionsWithTimetable === totalActiveSections`: Full section timetable schedule coverage.
    3. `pricedFeeHeads === totalActiveFeeHeads`: Fee structures with non-zero prices set.
    4. `activeTeacherAllocations > 0`: Active teacher-subject allocations in `sectionSubjectTeachers`.
    5. `enrolledStudents > 0`: Active student roster in `students`.
  - `isOperationallyReady`: Strictly guarded to 100% operational score (all 5 milestones green).
  - Maintained `overallScore` as backward-compatible alias to `configurationScore`.
  - Parallelized execution of all 13 tenant health queries via `Promise.all`.

- **F15: Setup Hub Dual-Score UI & Category Cards (`school-setup/page.tsx` & `settings/page.tsx`)**:
  - Setup Hub UI (`school-setup/page.tsx`):
    - Rendered responsive 2-panel scorecard grid for `Configuration Completeness` and `Operational Readiness`.
    - Rendered 5-gate responsive checklist strip (`grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-5`) with visual indicators for Bell Schedule, Timetable Coverage, Fee Pricing, Teacher Allocations, and Student Enrollment.
    - Added `Timetable & Bell Schedule` card linking to `/academics/timetable` with live metrics (`X Periods • Y/Z Scheduled`).
    - Added `Enrollment & Allocations` card linking to `/students` and `/academics` with live metrics (`X Students • Y Allocations`).
  - Settings Dashboard (`settings/page.tsx`):
    - Added top overview banner displaying dual score meters (Configuration Completeness + Operational Readiness) with direct navigation link to Setup Hub.
  - Universal scroll-and-click compliance: zero horizontal overflow at 390px mobile breakpoint, touch targets >= 38px.

- **F16: Fee Structures Pricing Metric & Next-Best Action Alert**:
  - Fee structures category metric reports priced vs unpriced fee heads (`${pricedFeeHeadsCount}/${feeHeadsCount} Heads Priced (${unpricedFeeHeadsCount} Unpriced)`).
  - Zero-price fee heads clamp status strictly to `PARTIAL`, never `COMPLETE`, whenever `pricedFeeHeads < totalActiveFeeHeads`.
  - Prominent Next-Best-Action alert card rendered on Setup Hub when unpriced heads exist, linking directly to `/finance/fees`.
  - In-card contextual Next-Best-Action button on the Fee Structures card linking to `/finance/fees`.

- **F17: `setupSchool` Academic Year Singularity Guard**:
  - Transactionally deactivates existing active academic years (`isActive: false`) before activating the target year.
  - Enforces that exactly one academic year is active per tenant school at all times.
  - Idempotently upserts target academic year on existing label collision to avoid unique constraint violations (`academic_years_school_label_unique`).

## 2. Invariants & Security Architecture

### 2.1 Dual-Score Orthogonality (Honesty Law AC-4 & AC-5)
- A blueprint-provisioned digital twin achieves 100% `configurationScore`, yet reports 0% `operationalScore` until bell periods, timetables, fee prices, teacher allocations, and student rosters are persisted.
- Eliminates false readiness claims by strictly separating static metadata from live operational capability.

### 2.2 Strict 100% Operational Readiness Invariant
- A school with 4/5 milestones complete achieves 80% `operationalScore` and `isOperationallyReady === false`.
- The system never certifies day-one readiness if a single operational milestone is unmet.

### 2.3 Fee Pricing Honesty & Partial Clamp Invariant
- The existence of fee heads without prices does not grant completion. Unpriced heads force `PARTIAL` status and surface next-best-action remediation.

### 2.4 Academic Year Singularity Invariant
- Eliminates multi-session ambiguity across attendance, timetable, and billing queries by guaranteeing $\sum [isActive = true] \le 1$ per tenant.

### 2.5 Fail-Closed RBAC & Session Scoping
- `setupSchool` restricted strictly to `SUPER_ADMIN` and `SCHOOL_ADMIN`.
- `getSchoolConfigurationHealthAction` restricted to `SUPER_ADMIN`, `SCHOOL_ADMIN`, and `PRINCIPAL`.
- Tenant scope strictly established from server session (`requireSchool(ctx)`).

### 2.6 Universal Scroll-and-Click Law (R7)
- Setup Hub and Settings Dashboard maintain zero horizontal overflow at 390px mobile viewport.
- All touch targets enforce minimum height >= 38px.

## 1. Implementation Summary

`invalidateTeachingStaffCache(school.id)` was systematically wired into all 8 staff and designation mutation actions in `frontend/src/app/(admin)/hr/actions.ts`:

1. **`createDesignation`**: Clears cache immediately after new designation insertion.
2. **`updateDesignation`**: Clears cache immediately after designation updates (including `isTeaching` flips).
3. **`archiveDesignation`**: Clears cache immediately after designation deactivation/activation.
4. **`deleteDesignation`**: Clears cache immediately after designation deletion.
5. **`createStaff`**: Clears cache immediately after staff onboarding and credentials creation.
6. **`updateStaff`**: Clears cache immediately after staff profile and designation assignments.
7. **`offboardStaff`**: Clears cache immediately after employee separation and force-reassignment unlinking.
8. **`reactivateStaff`**: Clears cache immediately after previously separated staff member reactivation.

## 3. BullMQ Queue Producer Findings (L0 Open Verification Item)

**Investigation**:
- Searched codebase for producers enqueuing `FLUSH_QUIET_HOURS` or `DRAIN_SMS` jobs into the `"exam-notifications"` BullMQ queue.
- **Finding**: While `notificationQueue` and the BullMQ worker are defined in `notificationQueueWorker.ts`, **no scheduler producer exists anywhere in the codebase** to enqueue `FLUSH_QUIET_HOURS` or `DRAIN_SMS` jobs. The queue is currently write-only and dormant.
- **Resolution Plan (Layer L4)**:
  1. Add `registerNotificationSchedulers()` in `backend/src/workers/notificationQueueWorker.ts` using BullMQ repeatable jobs (daily quiet-hours flush at 07:05 IST, periodic SMS drain every 15 minutes).
  2. Provide a webhook/cron route `frontend/src/app/api/cron/notifications/route.ts` protected by `CRON_SECRET` for serverless or external scheduler triggers.
  3. Document `START_WORKERS=true`, Redis/Valkey, and VAPID prerequisites in deployment documentation.

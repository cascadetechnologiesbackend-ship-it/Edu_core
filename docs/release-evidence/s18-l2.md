2. **Auto-seeding of Active Sections**: Automatically queries active sections of the class and seeds `sectionSubjectTeachers` rows with `isPrimary: true`, `academicYearId`, `periodsPerWeek`, and `effectiveFrom` for sections without an existing override.
3. **Displacement Capture**: Captures `oldTeacherId` from `classSubjects` before updating.
4. **Dual Cache Invalidation**: Calls `invalidateTeachingStaffCache(schoolId)`, `clearTeacherScopeCache(oldTeacherId)`, and `clearTeacherScopeCache(newTeacherId)`.
5. **Caller Independence**: Call sites (`saveClassSubject` in Academics and `hrAssignSubjectTeacher` in HR) maintain their own role guards, audit logs, and path revalidations.

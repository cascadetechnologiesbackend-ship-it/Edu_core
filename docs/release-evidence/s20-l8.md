## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

Sprint S20 evolves SchoolMitra ERP into a configurable, production-ready **School Digital Twin** architecture while standardizing enterprise UI/UX components across administrative, academic, financial, HR, and platform workflows.

When a school onboards, SchoolMitra automatically provisions common configuration, reference data, operational policies, and editable templates across 8 domains (School Core, Academics, Fees & Accounts, HR & Payroll, Admissions & People, Operations, Communication & Compliance, Platform Capabilities). The school only needs to supply its actual students, staff, rooms, vehicles, and school-specific prices.

## 3. Implemented Architecture Layers

### Layer 1: Schema, Monotonic Migration 0055 & Entity Catalog
- Added `schoolProvisions` table and relations in `database/src/schema/superadmin.ts` with `sp_school_unique` unique constraint to enforce idempotency.
- Created `database/src/migrations/0055_school_provisions.sql` with statement breakpoints and composite indexes (`sp_school_idx`, `sp_profile_idx`).
- Registered in `database/src/migrations/meta/_journal.json` (`idx: 55`, timestamp `1795100000000`).
- Registered in `packages/entity-engine/src/catalog/entityGraph.ts` under domain `SUPERADMIN_OPS`.

### Layer 2: Canonical Blueprint Catalog (`frontend/src/lib/canonicalBlueprints.ts`)
- Defined immutable, versioned, curriculum-compliant school digital twin blueprints:
  1. **CBSE Secondary (Classes 1 - 10)**: 10 classes, 8 subjects, 6-day bell schedule, 6 fee heads, 3 salary grades, national holidays, system roles.
  2. **CBSE Primary (Nursery - Class 5)**: Foundational learning, 5-day schedule with play/activity breaks, early learning kits.
  3. **CBSE Senior Secondary (Classes 11 - 12)**: Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams with lab allocations.
  4. **ICSE Secondary (Classes 1 - 10)**: CISCE board curriculum with specialized English literature and science applications.
  5. **Karnataka State Board (Classes 1 - 10)**: Regional language medium, state syllabus, state gazetted holidays.
  6. **IB Primary & Middle Years (PYP/MYP)**: Inquiry-based units, interdisciplinary learning outcomes.

### Layer 3: Provisioning Engine & Dry-Run Preview (`frontend/src/lib/provisioningEngine.ts`)
- `dryRunProvisionSchool`: Non-mutating preview analyzing prospective classes, sections, subjects, fee heads, departments, holidays, and roles with existing vs prospective diff counts.
- `executeProvisionSchool`: Transactional provisioning with relative date resolution for academic cycles, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` customisation preservation, and provenance record lock in `school_provisions`.

### Layer 4: Enterprise UI/UX Component System (`frontend/src/components/ui/`)
- `PageHeader`: Accessible header with breadcrumbs, title, status pill, and action buttons.
- `MetricCard`: KPI card with trend indicator and icon.
- `SectionShell`: Standardized section wrapper with action slots.
- `StatusBadge`: Semantic status pills (Active, Pending, Completed, Error, Draft) with icons and text.
- `FormField`: Accessible form control wrapper with `<label>`, required asterisk, helper text, and inline error.
- `ActionMenu`: Accessible row dropdown powered by `@radix-ui/react-dropdown-menu`.
- `ConfirmationDialog`: High-impact confirmation modal with destructive phrase verification.
- `Drawer`: Slide-over sheet powered by `@radix-ui/react-dialog`.
- `SkeletonLoader`: Loading skeletons for metric cards and tables.
- `DataTable`: Full-featured accessible table with sorting, search filtering, and pagination using `@tanstack/react-table`.

### Layer 5: Super-Admin Provisioning & Blueprint Management
- `/super-admin/templates`: Blueprint matrix catalog with board filters, KPI cards, and pack inspection.
- `/super-admin/templates/[profileId]`: Pack detail inspector displaying classes, subjects, bell schedule, fee heads, salary grades, holidays, and roles.
- `/super-admin/schools`: Integrated "Provision" button and `SchoolProvisionModal` for tenant-scoped dry-run preview and atomic digital twin execution.
- Updated `PlatformSidebar` with "Digital Twins & Blueprints" navigation.

### Layer 6: School Admin Configuration Hub (`/settings/school-setup`)
- Segmented Tab Switcher: `[Setup Hub & Digital Twin]` and `[Institution Profile & Branding]`.
- Digital Twin Provenance Banner with blueprint version and timestamp.
- Persisted Operational Health Completeness Score (0-100%) computed dynamically from real database records across 6 operational milestones.
- Grouped Category Cards with live metrics linking to domain workspaces:
  - Academic Calendar & Terms
  - Classes & Sections Hierarchy
  - Curriculum & Subject Catalog
  - Timetable & Bell Schedule
  - Fee Heads & Pricing Structures
  - HR Salary Templates
- Updated `/settings` dashboard with discovery cards.

### Layer 7: Timetable Scaffold & Workload Feasibility Engine (`frontend/src/lib/timetableEngine.ts`)
- `validateWorkloadFit`: Mathematical workload fit validator ($\sum \text{periods} \le \text{workingDays} \times \text{periods/day}$).
- `generateDeterministicSchedule`: Balanced provisional schedule generation distributing subjects across days (max 2 periods of same subject per day), preserving manual overrides.
- `checkScheduleClashes`: Concurrency conflict detection for teacher double-booking and room overlap.

### Layer 8: Universal Import / Export Pipeline (`frontend/src/lib/importExportEngine.ts`, `ImportExportModal.tsx`)
- Schema definitions for 7 datasets: `STUDENTS`, `STAFF`, `CLASSES`, `SUBJECTS`, `FEE_HEADS`, `SALARY_TEMPLATES`, `HOLIDAYS`.
- `generateCsvTemplate`: Pre-formatted downloadable CSV templates with headers and sample data.
- `validateImportBatch`: Row-level dry-run validator with line number error reporting and batch duplicate key detection.
- `generateRejectedRowsCsv`: Generates a downloadable CSV containing only failed rows with appended error reasons for rapid spreadsheet correction.
- `sanitizeExportRecord`: DPDP & PCI compliant export sanitizer redacting passwords, bank details, tokens, and PINs.
- `ImportExportModal`: 4-step modal workflow (Template $\rightarrow$ Upload CSV $\rightarrow$ Dry-run Preview $\rightarrow$ Commit).

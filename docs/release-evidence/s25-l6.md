## 1. Summary of Changes

Layer L6 implements the live, tenant-scoped Admin Delivery Health surface at `frontend/src/app/(admin)/admin/notifications/delivery/page.tsx` and re-exports it at `frontend/src/app/(admin)/communication/delivery/page.tsx`, fulfilling **S25-F07** and **S25 AC-8**:

1. **Live Tenant-Scoped 7-Day Outcome Telemetry**:
   - Aggregates real `pushNotificationLogs` outcomes inside `withTenant(schoolId)`: `DELIVERED`, `FAILED`, `DEFERRED_QUIET_HOURS`, `SKIPPED_UNCONFIGURED`.
   - Aggregates real `smsLogs` outcomes: `SENT`, `FAILED`, `QUEUED`.
   - Never fabricates delivery percentages or hardcoded mock statistics.
2. **Channel Configuration Diagnostics & Warning Banners**:
   - Inspects `isWebPushConfigured()`, `isSmsConfigured()`, and `isEmailConfigured()`.
   - Displays prominent warning cards when keys or credentials are missing with remediation hints (`npx web-push generate-vapid-keys`, DLT credentials, SMTP host).
   - Displays clean all-active confirmation when all 3 channels are ready.
3. **Daily SMS Quota Utilization Bar**:
   - Renders live progress bar against the 500 SMS/day quota (`smsTodaySent / 500`).
   - Counts strictly verified `SENT` messages; unconfigured or failed messages do not consume school allocation.
4. **Universal Mobile Law & Accessibility Compliance**:
   - Viewport tested at 390px mobile width.
   - Root container enforces `overflow-x-hidden`.
   - Zero `<table>` elements on page; all logs reflow vertically as individual card components.
   - All interactive touch targets meet or exceed $\ge 38$px (`min-h-[38px]`, `min-w-[38px]`).
5. **Strict RBAC**:
   - Enforces `SCHOOL_ADMIN`, `SUPER_ADMIN`, and `PRINCIPAL` access; redirects unprivileged roles (`STUDENT`, `PARENT`, `TEACHER`) to `/unauthorized`.

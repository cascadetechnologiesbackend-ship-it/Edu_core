## 2. Flaw Closure: S23-F05 (Driver App Fabricated Identity & Route Theft)

Prior to Layer 5, `(driver)/driver/dashboard/page.tsx` contained a fallback that:
- Fabricated a fake driver identity: `id: "demo"`, name `"School Bus Driver"`, licence `"DL-DEMO-2026"`.
- Fabricated route assignment by arbitrarily stealing the first route in the school table (`assignedRoute = await db.query.routes.findFirst(...)`).

This was a direct violation of the Honesty Law.

### Closure Implementation:
- Permanently eliminated `DL-DEMO-2026`, `id: "demo"`, and arbitrary `routes.findFirst` fallback route theft.
- When an authenticated user has no linked driver profile, or no assigned vehicle, or no assigned transit route:
  - Renders an honest, dignified setup-missing card (`data-testid="driver-setup-missing-card"`).
  - Displays clear status: "No Vehicle or Route Assigned" and "Transport Allocation Pending".
  - Provides clear instructions: "Please contact the Transport Operations Office to register your commercial driving licence and mobile number."
  - For administrators/transport managers previewing the page, provides a direct action link to `/transport` to configure fleet vehicles and driver routes.
- When legitimately assigned, decrypts the real driver profile name and licence and renders the active `DriverDashboardClient`.

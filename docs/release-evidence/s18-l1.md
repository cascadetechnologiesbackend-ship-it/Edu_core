## 1. Resolution & Policy Decision (OD1)

Per approved user decision on **OD1**:
- `PRINCIPAL` is retained in `revealStaffPii` (`requireAuth(["HR_MANAGER", "SUPER_ADMIN", "SCHOOL_ADMIN", "PRINCIPAL"])`), formally amending the prior OD2 directory matrix.
- `PRINCIPAL` requires operational access to verify employee statutory records (PAN, Bank details) during school operations and audits.
- **Mandatory DPDP Act 2023 Compliance**:
  - Every reveal writes an unredacted audit log entry to `auditLogs` through `logHrAudit(ctx, "READ", "staff", staffId, { revealedField: field })`.
  - Non-administrative roles (`TEACHER`, `PARENT`, `STUDENT`) are strictly rejected with 403 Forbidden.
  - Multi-tenant boundary is enforced: Principals and HR managers of School A cannot decrypt or view staff of School B.

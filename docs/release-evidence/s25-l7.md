## 1. Summary of Walkthrough Verification

The Layer L7 verification suite (`backend/src/lib/__tests__/s25-walkthrough.test.ts`) simulates the complete notification delivery lifecycle under production and adverse boundary conditions, proving that zero fabricated metrics or cross-recipient leaks can occur:

### Act 1: Exam Result Release in Daytime
- **Setup**: Triggered `notifyResultReleased` for an exam with two student parents (Parent A with active Web Push subscription, Parent B with no subscription).
- **Inverted Write-Path**: `dispatchWebPush` was invoked *before* any database push log was created.
- **Log Truth**: Database logs received exact, individual outcome rows:
  - Parent A: `DELIVERED`
  - Parent B: `SKIPPED_NO_SUBSCRIPTION`
- **Tally Truth**: `pushSentCount` reported by the service was strictly 1 (reflecting `DELIVERED` only), completely eliminating the pre-S25 flaw of reporting `pushSentCount = recipientList.length`.

### Act 2: Quiet Hours Deferral
- **Setup**: Triggered `notifyAdmitCardsReleased` during quiet hours (22:30 IST).
- **Execution**: Web Push dispatch was completely bypassed; push logs were inserted with `status = "DEFERRED_QUIET_HOURS"`.
- **Payload Preservation**: Individual parent recipient IDs and distinct notice payloads were saved cleanly without dispatching.

### Act 3: Morning Flush & Payload Integrity
- **Setup**: Simulated scheduled flush at 07:05 IST outside quiet hours with 2 deferred notifications containing different exam titles and bodies.
- **Execution**: `flushQuietHoursNotifications` grouped by exact `(title, body)` and dispatched distinct payloads to respective parents.
- **Outcome**: Verified zero cross-recipient content leaking; statuses were updated to `DELIVERED` only after successful dispatch.

### Act 4: SMS Queue Drain & Quota Enforcement
- **Setup**: Queued SMS log for Parent A with encrypted mobile number in `users`.
- **Execution**: `drainQueuedSms` decrypted the mobile number, invoked `sendSMSWithStatus`, transitioned status to `SENT`, recorded the gateway message ID (`gw-sms-998877`), and decremented the 500/day school quota.
- **Missing Data Safety**: Recipients without phone numbers failed honestly (`status = "FAILED"`) without fabricating `SENT`.

### Act 5: Fail-Closed Truth Under Unconfigured Production
- **Web Push**: Without VAPID keys, returns `SKIPPED_UNCONFIGURED`.
- **SMS**: Without SMS credentials, defers in `QUEUED` state without consuming quota or faking `SENT`.
- **Email**: Without SMTP credentials in production, returns `{ delivered: false, unconfigured: true }` without throwing or connecting to `smtp.example.com`.
